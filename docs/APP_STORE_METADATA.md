### Description

```
Hanzi Stroke Practice — type Chinese characters, get printable stroke-order worksheets in seconds.

Need stroke order for homework? Want neat mizi-grid sheets without drawing grids by hand? Enter the characters you want to practice. The app builds an A4-ready sheet: one new stroke per cell with the latest stroke highlighted in red, plus tracing and blank copy grids you can print.

【KEY FEATURES】
• Progressive stroke cells — one additional stroke per box; new stroke in red
• Mizi-grid layout — semi-transparent tracing + blank practice cells (customizable 0–10 each)
• Multiple characters — one row per character; great for weekly character lists
• Print & PDF — system print, save to Files, or share PDF for A4 printing
• Built-in dictionary — thousands of characters with stroke data for everyday use
• Recent sheets — reopen and reprint your last generated sheets locally

【WHO IT'S FOR】
• Students and parents learning correct Chinese stroke order
• Teachers preparing handwriting homework quickly
• Heritage learners and self-study Chinese learners worldwide

【PRIVACY】
• No account required
• Processing is primarily on your device
• No ads

【FREE TRIAL & UNLOCK】
• Free: 20 successful sheet generations included
• Share bonus: share the app from Settings for +5 generations per share (up to 10 shares)
• One-time purchase: unlimited generations forever; Restore Purchases supported

【NOTE】
Stroke data is based on the open-source Make Me a Hanzi project. Rare characters may not be in the dictionary; the app will notify you when characters are missing.

Download now and spend less time making worksheets — more time practicing.
```

## 五、填写注意事项

| 项目 | 说明 |
|------|------|
| 描述前 3 行 | 用户不点「更多」也能看到；草稿已将卖点写在前段 |
| 关键词 | 不要重复 App 名称里的字；100 字符用满但不要超长 |
| 截图文案 | 可与副标题、描述前几句对齐，强化「笔顺 / 米字格 / PDF」 |
| 定价 | 描述里未写具体价格（避免改价后文案过时）；审核备注写清 20 次免费 + 一次性解锁 |
| App Store URL | 上架后替换 `lib/src/config/app_share_config.dart` 与 `website/hp/index.html` 中的搜索链接 |
